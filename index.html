About the project

Emergencies such as traffic accidents, earthquakes or injuries at home often happen when no health professional is nearby, and the first minutes matter most. People who are on the scene usually want to help, but they do not know how serious the situation is or what they should do. In disasters, mobile networks may also be down, so online services and remote help cannot be relied on.

TriaTED is a mobile application that anyone can use, without medical training and without an internet connection. Using the phone's camera together with on-device image processing and AI models, it helps assess the injured person's condition, estimates how urgent the situation is, and guides the user step by step through the right first-aid actions in plain language. All processing happens on the device, so the app keeps working when there is no network. TriaTED supports, and does not replace, professional emergency services, and reminds the user to call 112 when needed.

Works offline

Everything runs on the device, with no internet or mobile network needed.
