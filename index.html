About the project

alerTED is an offline, AI-powered mobile decision-support application designed for emergency situations such as disasters, conflicts, and operations in remote areas where internet access may be limited or unavailable.

The application assists users, including those without formal medical training, in assessing casualties through clear, step-by-step questions covering vital status and observable signs of traumatic injury. Using the device’s camera, its on-device AI analyzes visible injuries such as open wounds, burns, and active bleeding, providing additional information to support assessment.

alerTED combines clinical information and visual analysis with established first aid and triage protocols to provide appropriate first aid guidance and support casualty prioritization in multi-casualty situations. Both AI-based image analysis and decision-support functions operate entirely on the device, without requiring an internet connection.

alerTED is currently being developed and evaluated as a research project under controlled conditions.
